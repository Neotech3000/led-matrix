Left cards drive the left well, right cards the right well. L/R badges send a loop to either side. Heart a card to favorite it. Click a group name to view it. The + on a group enters selection mode so you can add many animations without opening the group. Keyboard goes to the well you last clicked.

Not an official Framework product. MIT licensed — built for people who just bought a Framework 16 and want the LED wells to do something.
